## Chapter 01 — The Last Light On

Night Shift. Chapter one. The last light on.

By the time the town went dark, the observatory was only beginning to wake. Mara set a cup of tea beside the keyboard and opened the night's folder. Photographs, recordings, diagrams, and a note from the previous shift. Everything was there. She just needed to look.

The first photograph showed the ridge beneath a pale moon. A single window glowed in the dome. In the next room, the receiver clicked once, then settled into a quiet rhythm.

Mara pressed play. A soft pattern of notes filled the room. She kept the music running and opened the field guide. Halfway down the page was a diagram of the station's signal path. Below it, a photograph of the antenna at daybreak.

At the bottom of the note, someone had written five words. Keep listening after the silence.

She read them again. Outside, a small light moved steadily across the sky.

## Chapter 02 — A Signal Worth Keeping

Night Shift. Chapter two. A signal worth keeping.

The light was an ordinary satellite. Mara checked the time, marked the pass, and returned to the recording. Somewhere between the static and the wind was a repeating interval. Three notes, a pause, then three notes again.

She moved back along the timeline. This time she slowed the playback. The pattern was still there, small enough to miss and clear enough to recognize once she knew where to listen.

Mara made a new note. Not a discovery, not yet. Just a signal worth keeping. She placed the recording beside the photographs and drew a line from the receiver to the archive on her diagram.

At dawn, the mountains changed from blue to copper. She saved the last image and closed the guide. The music had ended. The room was quiet.

Before leaving, Mara added one sentence for the next shift. Everything you need is in this folder. Take a look.
