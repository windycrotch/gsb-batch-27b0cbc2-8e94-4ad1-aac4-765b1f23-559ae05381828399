# About this collection

Night Shift is original demonstration content made for peep. The observatory,
Mara, and the field notes are fictional. No personal media or third-party songs
are included.

- **Artwork:** original procedural illustrations, generated by `cmd/peep-demo-assets`.
- **Music:** original 40-second instrumental, 96 BPM, synthesized from a fixed score
  and deterministic noise. Pads, plucked melody, bass, and percussion are stereo.
- **Motion:** the original observatory illustration and satellite SVG, animated
  and encoded using FFmpeg. The GIF is silent; the MP4 has music.
- **Audiobook:** two original short chapters. Synthetic narration uses the macOS
  Samantha voice through `say`; it is not a recording of a human narrator.
- **Cover, logo, diagrams, and text:** original peep demo material. SVG sources
  and narration transcripts are included.

The encoded media is committed for immediate use on macOS or Linux. Rebuilding
is optional: `just demo-assets` needs Go, FFmpeg, and rsvg-convert. To also
regenerate the narration on macOS, run `NARRATE=1 just demo-assets`.

`just demo` copies the collection into a new ignored temporary workspace before
opening peep. Use that copy for destructive-operation demos. Ordinary builds and
installations do not generate or download demo content.
